TOP OF FUNNEL

BROAD REACH
PERSONALITY & STORY
Relatable trader moments, POV content, personal journey clips. Makes him a person people want to follow — not just another trading account. This is what got him followers in the first place. Keep it in rotation.
TRADING CULTURE
Broad market reactions, trading humour, hot takes on the space. Anyone who trades or wants to trade can connect with it. Low barrier — drives new follows from outside his current audience.
TALKING HEAD

MIDDLE OF FUNNEL
Credibility — Earn Their Respect
DEPTH & TRUST
PERSONAL P&L
Real results. Wins and losing days both. The transparency is the content — showing a down session builds more trust than only posting wins. Authority through honesty, not highlights.
CONCEPT BREAKDOWNS
One concept per video — delta divergence, orderflow reads, optionsflow. The kind of content serious traders save. Positions him as someone who understands the deeper mechanics. Not surface-level education.

BOTTOM OF FUNNEL
Conversion — Turn Followers Into Buyers
PROOF & OFFER
STUDENT RESULTS
When students hit milestones — post it. Screenshots, reactions, messages. At this follower count, social proof from real people carries more weight than any polished promo. This is the content that converts.
OFFER ADJACENT
Content that makes the offer visible without pitching it. "This is what I teach" clips, behind the scenes of a mentorship session, a student's first week. The offer becomes clear naturally — no hard sell needed.
SOFT CTA
One line at the end of every MOF and BOF piece. Nothing salesy — "link in bio if you want to learn this properly" is enough. The content does the convincing. The CTA just opens the door.
FORMAT ROTATION
01
TALKING HEAD
Face to camera, no charts. Opinion, take, personal story. The format that builds the most personal connection. Can't be faked.
02
SCREEN RECORD
Chart on screen, voice over. Best for breakdowns and trade recaps. Fast, repeatable, and lets the numbers speak.
03
SPLIT SCREEN
Face alongside chart or footage. Adds personality to technical content. Stops the scroll better than screen record alone.
04
TEXT ON SCREEN
B-roll or static visual, text overlay does the talking. High save rate. Works well for process, checklist, and opinion content.
05
POV / SKIT
The brainrot format that already works for him. Keep it in rotation for reach and follows — don't let it dominate the feed.

CONTENT IDEAS — YOUTUBE
TOF STORY
The personal journey video — where he started, what he tried, what didn't work, where he is now
Narrative-driven. No charts needed. The kind of video that gets shared because people see themselves in it. Builds the top of funnel on YouTube — new viewers find this and stay because they connect with the person before the method.
TOF RELATABLE
A video built around a moment every trader has experienced — the kind that gets comments saying "this is me"
Broad appeal. Lower production pressure. Drives subscribers from outside his current audience through relatability and algorithm reach. Keeps the channel discoverable without compromising the depth of the core content.
MOF RESULTS
Full session walkthrough — real trade, real numbers, what he saw before entering and why
P&L on screen. The orderflow and optionsflow read shown in real time. Authority through transparency. This is the video that converts watchers into people who want to learn exactly what he's doing.
MOF EDUCATIONAL
Deep dive on one concept he uses daily — the kind of thing most traders don't know exists
Dense and specific. Orderflow, footprint, delta, optionsflow. Attracts serious traders who are already searching for an edge. Positions him as someone operating at a different level — not just explaining common knowledge.
MOF BREAKDOWN
Why a common approach fails without the context he trades with — contrarian, specific, opinionated
Positions his method against the noise. ICT alone, supply/demand alone, indicators alone — and why layering changes everything. Builds authority by challenging what his audience already believes they know.
MOF PROCESS
Pre-market to close — the actual morning routine before he touches the market
DITL for traders. Shows the discipline and the system behind the results. Makes the mentorship feel like access to something real — not a course, a methodology. This video is a window, not a lesson.
BOF RESULTS
Monthly P&L review — honest accounting of wins, losses, and what actually moved the needle
No filter. Losing months included. This single content type builds more long-term trust than any highlight series. Viewers who stick around for these are the most conversion-ready audience on the channel.
BOF OFFER ADJACENT
Inside look at what his students learn — the framework, the first week, the methodology in practice
Not a sales video. A transparency video. The offer becomes visible naturally — no pitch required. The most converting YouTube content type for a mentorship. People watch this and they're already sold by the time the CTA appears.
SERVER STRUCTURE

The Discord is the bridge between free and paid. It doesn't need extra content — it runs off what's already being created. The job is consistency and showing up. Free members who stay long enough convert. These are the four channels that make that happen.

daily-prep

Brief daily post before market open. Bias, key levels, anything notable on the options flow. Free members get the framework without the full context. The habit of checking this channel is what retains them in the server long enough to convert.
DAILY
📊
trade-recaps
Session Breakdowns
→ SHOW THE STANDARD IN REAL TIME
Post-session. What traded, what worked, what didn't. Screenshots from the footprint. Honest — good days and bad. This channel builds more trust than anything else in the server because it's real, unfiltered output from a serious operator.
AFTER SESSIONS
🏆
student-results
Social Proof
→ LET THE RESULTS DO THE SELLING
Milestones from paid students. Screenshots, messages, P&L wins. Free members see this every time a student progresses. It's the primary conversion mechanism in the server — no selling required, no DMs needed. The proof does the work.
AS THEY COME IN
🔗
mentorship
Offer Visibility
→ ALWAYS VISIBLE, NEVER PUSHED
Pinned. Always accessible. Details, pricing, what's included — updated when the launch goes live. Every member who stays long enough will see it. No need to push it anywhere else. The other channels warm them up. This channel closes.
PINNED
Beyond these four channels, what gets built in the server should come from what resonates with his community — not from a template. These are the foundation.
